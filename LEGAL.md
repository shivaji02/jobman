# Security & Legal Notices

## Password Security

- This bot **NEVER** stores passwords in files (`.env`, JSON, or logs)
- It reuses browser sessions saved after you run `npm run login` once
- Only session cookies live in the local Chrome profile (`~/.jobman-profile` or legacy `.chrome-profile/`)
- If your account is compromised, change your password immediately on that portal

## Terms of Service

- Using automation may violate job portal Terms of Service
- Portals can ban or restrict your account without warning
- We are not liable for bans, suspensions, or lost opportunities

## Responsibility

- Use this bot at your own risk
- Review applications where possible; prefer `--dry-run` while testing
- Always follow portal guidelines and applicable law
- Never fabricate salary, certifications, or experience in application answers

## Reporting Security Issues

If you find a vulnerability (e.g. credentials written to disk), open an issue or contact the maintainer privately before public disclosure.
